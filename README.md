## 📌 What Is This Toolkit?

Building AI agents can be overwhelming. With hundreds of frameworks, libraries, models, and tools available, knowing where to start — and what to use — isn't always easy.

**The Open Source Toolkit for Building AI Agents** brings the entire AI agent ecosystem together in one comprehensive, easy-to-navigate resource.

📊 **What's Inside?**

- 📚 **400+ Entries** covering the open-source AI agent ecosystem.
- 📑 **20 Organized Sheets** for easy navigation and discovery.
- 🔍 **Detailed Tool Information** including purpose, target audience, license, and working links.
- 🛠️ **Open-Source & Open-Weight Resources** to explore, run, customize, and build with.
- 🗺️ **Learning Roadmaps, Stack Recipes, Projects, and Interview Questions** to help you move from learning to building.

Whether you're exploring AI agents for the first time or developing production-ready systems, this toolkit helps you find the right resources without endless tutorial hopping.

> 💡 **The goal:** One resource to help you decide what to learn, what to install, and what to build next.

## 🚀 How to Use This Toolkit

Choose the path that matches your current goal.

### 1️⃣ New to AI Agents?

Start with **Sheet 16 — The 12-Week Roadmap**.

- Follow the roadmap step by step.
- Learn concepts in the recommended order.
- Explore the catalog sheets only when the roadmap directs you to them.

**Best for:** Beginners who want a structured learning path without information overload.

### 2️⃣ Building an AI Agent Right Now?

Head to **Sheet 15 — Stack Recipes**.

- Choose a stack that matches your budget and requirements.
- Look up each recommended tool in its corresponding catalog sheet.
- Compare alternatives before deciding what to install.

**Best for:** Developers who want to move from an idea to a working AI agent.

### 3️⃣ Preparing for AI Agent Interviews?

Use the dedicated interview preparation resources.

- **Sheet 18:** Practice 40 interview questions.
- **Sheet 17:** Explore 14 hands-on projects.
- Build at least two projects and explain your design decisions aloud.

**Best for:** Developers preparing for AI engineering, agent development, and related technical interviews.

### 4️⃣ Choosing the Right Tool?

Use the catalog sheets to compare your options.

- Filter tools by **Difficulty** and **Adoption**.
- Compare alternatives based on your project requirements.
- When two tools are otherwise comparable, consider choosing the one with stronger adoption and community support.
- Prioritize good documentation, reliable support, and an active ecosystem over feature count alone.

**Best for:** Engineers making informed technology and architecture decisions.

### 5️⃣ Navigating the Toolkit Efficiently

Make the most of the spreadsheet's built-in features.

- 🔗 Every **Link** column contains clickable links.
- 🔎 Use header-row filters to find relevant tools quickly.
- 📌 Frozen panes keep important headers visible while scrolling.
- 📂 Browse the 20 sheets to explore different parts of the AI agent ecosystem.

**Tip:** Filter first, compare second, and experiment before committing to a tool.
